# Kestrel VPN Access Policy (v1, deprecated)

This document is the **original** Kestrel Corp remote-access VPN policy. It
has been superseded by "Kestrel VPN Access Policy (v2)" and is retained
here only for historical reference. Do not follow these instructions for
current VPN setup.

## Original Client Software

Employees were required to install the legacy "KestrelConnect Classic"
VPN client, version 3.x, available from the internal software portal.
Passwords were reset by emailing the Security team's shared inbox and
waiting up to two business days for a manual reset.

## Original Password Rules

VPN passwords under this policy were valid for 180 days and had no
minimum length requirement beyond 8 characters. Multi-factor
authentication was optional, not required, for standard employees.

## Why This Policy Was Retired

The 180-day rotation window and optional MFA were flagged in a 2024
internal security review as insufficient for a remote-access system with
access to production infrastructure. See the v2 policy for the current
rules, which shortened the rotation window, made MFA mandatory, and moved
password resets to a self-service flow.
