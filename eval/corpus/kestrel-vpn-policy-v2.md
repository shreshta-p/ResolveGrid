# Kestrel VPN Access Policy (v2)

This is the **current** Kestrel Corp remote-access VPN policy, effective
2025-06-01. It supersedes "Kestrel VPN Access Policy (v1, deprecated)" in
full. All employees connecting to internal systems remotely must follow
these rules.

## Client Software

Install "KestrelConnect" (current major version, distributed through the
internal software portal) on any device used for remote access. The
legacy "KestrelConnect Classic" client is no longer supported and will be
blocked from connecting after its end-of-life date.

## Password Reset

To reset your VPN password, open the KestrelConnect client and select
"Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen. This sends a one-time reset link
to your Kestrel email address; the self-service flow completes in
minutes, replacing the old email-the-Security-team process. If you no
longer have access to your Kestrel email, contact the IT Support desk for
identity verification.

## Password and MFA Rules

VPN passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 90
days. Multi-factor authentication (a push notification to the Kestrel
Authenticator app) is mandatory for every VPN session, with no
opt-out, for all employees and contractors.

## Access Scope

VPN access grants routes only to the internal systems a given role
requires (least-privilege routing), not a flat connection to the entire
corporate network. Access scope is tied to your department and any
project-specific grants your manager has approved.
