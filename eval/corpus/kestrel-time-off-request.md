# Kestrel People Ops Time-Off Request Policy

This policy explains how Kestrel Corp employees request and track paid
time off (PTO) through People Ops.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit a time-off request through the internal HR portal at least 5
business days before the requested start date for planned leave. Requests
under 3 days are auto-approved once your manager acknowledges the
request; longer requests require explicit manager sign-off before they
are confirmed.

## PTO Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at a fixed rate per pay period based on
tenure, up to an annual cap. Unused PTO up to 40 hours carries over into
the next calendar year; any balance above that is forfeited at year-end
unless local law requires otherwise.

## Sick Leave and Emergency Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not require advance
notice, only notification to your manager as soon as practical. Emergency
leave requests (bereavement, family emergencies) are handled the same
way; People Ops can backfill the paperwork after the fact.

## Questions and Disputes

Direct questions about accrual balances, denied requests, or policy
exceptions to the People Ops queue rather than to your manager directly,
so there is a consistent record of how exceptions are handled.
