# Kestrel Laptop Provisioning and Offboarding Policy

This policy covers how the IT Support team issues, images, and reclaims
Kestrel Corp laptops for employees and contractors.

## New Hire Provisioning

IT Support images a new laptop with the standard Kestrel software bundle
(OS, endpoint security agent, KestrelConnect VPN client, and
department-specific tooling) before an employee's start date. New hires
pick up their laptop from the Austin HQ IT desk, or receive it by courier
for remote hires, no later than their first day.

## Loaner Devices

A loaner laptop is available from the IT desk for employees whose primary
device is being repaired. Loaners are pre-imaged with the same standard
bundle and must be returned within 10 business days or the request must
be renewed with a manager's approval.

## Offboarding and Device Return

When an employee leaves Kestrel Corp, IT Support disables their device's
VPN and single sign-on access on their last working day. The laptop must
be returned to IT Support (in person or by prepaid courier for remote
employees) within 5 business days of departure. Devices not returned
within 30 days are reported to Security as a potential asset-recovery
issue.

## Support Requests

For laptop hardware issues, software installation requests, or questions
about this policy, open a ticket with the IT Support queue rather than
contacting individual technicians directly, so requests are tracked and
prioritized consistently.
